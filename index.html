Frameless, semi-frameless, or framed?

The three styles, honestly compared. Your specialist will confirm what fits your opening, studs, and budget.

Frameless

Thick ⅜″–½″ tempered glass with minimal clips and hinges. The clean, spa look — nothing between you and the tile. Needs solid mounting surfaces and precise measuring, which is why installers template it first.

Typically the premium option

Semi-frameless

Frameless-style door with slim framing where structure is needed. Most of the look, a friendlier price, and a little more forgiving on older or out-of-square openings.

The middle ground most people pick

Framed

Thinner glass in a full metal frame. The most economical and the sturdiest choice for high-use family bathrooms, rentals, and guest suites.

The budget-friendly option

How long does a frameless enclosure take?

After a specialist measures (templates) your opening, custom glass is typically cut and tempered in about 1–3 weeks, with installation usually taking 2–4 hours. Stock-size doors can be faster. Your quotes will include each specialist's current timeline.

Frameless vs. framed — which should I choose?

Frameless gives the open, high-end look and is easier to squeegee clean, but costs more and needs precise, plumb walls. Framed costs less, seals better in out-of-square openings, and holds up well in busy family bathrooms. Semi-frameless splits the difference. A good specialist will tell you honestly which your bathroom suits.

Will frameless glass leak?

A properly templated frameless enclosure uses seals and sweeps to manage water, but it is not a submarine hatch — tiny amounts of water at the door edge are normal in a hard spray. If your shower design sprays directly at the door, a specialist may recommend semi-frameless or framed for a tighter seal.

How many quotes will I get?

That depends on specialist availability in your city — typically one to three. We'd rather send your request to a few right-fit local shops than blast it to everyone.

What happens to my information?
